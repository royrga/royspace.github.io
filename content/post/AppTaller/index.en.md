## Description

Mobile application for Android phones that provides tools to manage your auto
shop's information.

### Features

- `Service Log`: Stores information on the services you perform at your shop
  (Customer Name, Date, Vehicle, Mileage, Services,
  Next Service Date, Safety Points, Images).

- `View Services`: View information on the services you have logged. You can search
  by Customer, Date, or Vehicle. It shows you the images you have saved.

- `Generate a Report`: Create a PDF report with service information to share with
  your customer.

## Upcoming Improvements

### Upcoming Features

- `Quote section`: Similar to the service registration tool,
  but to store quote information, you will enter data on parts costs and labor.
- `Schedule section`: Tool for scheduling services with date, time,
  customer name, and type of service. To keep track of and record upcoming services.
- `Customer app`: Allows customers to view their car's service log and receive
  notifications for tune-up services.

### Improvements

The app requires an internet connection to function, as service information and
images are backed up in the cloud. The storage strategy will soon be updated so
the app can function offline.

## About the app and its future

The current state of the app was achieved with feedback from a mechanic's shop
that is currently using it.

The developer's goal is to continue implementing improvements and new features
periodically based on user feedback.
